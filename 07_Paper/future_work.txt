Future Work

Future studies can extend this work in several directions.

First, the experiments can be repeated using multiple datasets from different domains to determine whether similar patterns are observed.

Second, future work can investigate different missing-data mechanisms instead of only randomly introducing missing values. For example, missing data can be generated under different assumptions about why values are missing.

Third, more machine learning models can be compared, including K-Nearest Neighbors, Support Vector Machines, and other classification algorithms.

Fourth, additional missing-data handling methods can be evaluated. These may include KNN imputation, iterative imputation, and multiple imputation methods.

Finally, future experiments can use additional evaluation metrics and different train-test splits or cross-validation to provide a more extensive evaluation of model performance.
